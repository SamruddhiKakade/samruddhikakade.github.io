## Problem

Students leaving campus throw away things others could use, while new students buy the same things new. A local
marketplace between students saves money and waste.

The product design called for posting items with a photo, price and condition, browsing by distance, messaging
sellers, verifying a university email address, reporting fraud, and planning an on-campus meet-up to hand items over.

## Approach

The team worked in two-week sprints, with the sprint leader role rotating so every member led in turn. We started on
Django, then moved the front end to React with Firebase.

## My part

- I set up the first backend: a Django project divided into apps for search, uploading items and registration, with
  its settings and template structure.
- I was the team's sprint leader for weeks 11 and 12.
