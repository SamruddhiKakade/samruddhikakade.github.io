## Context

HawkShop was the team project for IPRO 497 in Fall 2022, for a team of six: a web marketplace where students sell used
items to students nearby, to save money and reduce waste.

<Flow
  label="The team's versions of HawkShop, in order"
  steps={['Product design document', 'Django project with three apps', 'React and Node application', 'Firebase for stored items and images']}
/>

<More label="Planned capabilities and scenarios">
- **Capabilities.** The design document listed posting an item with photos, price and condition; browsing items within
  a set distance; messaging sellers; verifying users by university email; reporting fraud or damage; and agreeing a
  time and place on campus for the hand-over.
- **Scenarios.** A graduating student selling a refrigerator, a student selling textbooks to an incoming second-year
  student, and a new student searching for a used calculator.
- **Planned stack.** Django for the backend, React for the front end and an Oracle database.
</More>

## Approach

The team followed a rotating sprint-leader schedule in two-week blocks, and every member led at least once. Its first
backend was a Django project; the team then moved to a React and Node application, which later used Firebase to store
items and images.

<More label="The Django project and the sprint schedule">
- **Django project.** The project was split into three apps, for product search, product upload and user
  registration, and teammates then added the database tables and the search route.
- **Sprint schedule.** Weeks 11 and 12 fell in the phase that prepared the final demo. The team's focus then was
  uploading files, setting up and using the database, acceptance criteria for stories, and the stories needed for the
  demo.
</More>

## Contribution

- Configured the project's initial Django backend, with separate apps for product search, product upload and user
  registration.
- Served as sprint leader for weeks 11 and 12 of the team's rotating schedule, in the phase leading to the final demo.

## Lessons learned

- **An early split can outlast a change of stack.** The three areas defined in the Django project (search, upload and
  registration) reappear as components of the React application, so the early division of the product carried over.
- **Written acceptance criteria give continuity.** With the sprint leader changing every two weeks, acceptance
  criteria let each new leader and the rest of the team work from the same definition of done.
