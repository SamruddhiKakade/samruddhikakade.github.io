## Problem

Students need one place to reach each other, their professors, and support services such as health and wellness or
campus security.

<More>
The course brief asked for a "student assist" app. We designed ours for five roles (student, professor, health and
wellness staff, campus security and administrator). Anonymous messages to health and wellness and to campus security
were part of the design; the prototype focused on accounts, search and messaging.
</More>

## Approach

  label="How the team worked"

We split the work into front end, back end and database, and built a working prototype on Django and SQLite.

## My part

I wrote the backend: registration and login, searching for other users, and the messaging system.

<More>
- **Data model:** a chat between two users, and messages with a sender, a receiver, a body and a date.
- **Opening a chat:** the app first looks for an existing conversation between the two users, in either direction, before
  creating a new one.
- **Unread counts:** they go up for the other person when a message is sent and reset when a chat is opened.
- Every messaging page requires login, and I wired up the URL routing.
- I also suggested the technology the team built on.
</More>
