## Question

What did a video game advertisement of the 1980s teach players to expect, before they had touched the game?

## Argument

The advertisement for Demolition Derby frames the game as a contest of aggression: its image and its tagline invite
players to "throttle the competition". At a time when players had no reviews or videos to go by, the advertisement was
the game's first impression, and it shaped how the game was approached.

The essay closes by comparing this with today, when players can see reviews and gameplay instantly, and notes that
later advertising became more careful about the attitudes it promoted.

## How I approached it

I treated the advertisement as a historical source: what it shows, what it says, who it speaks to, and what it
leaves out.

The advertisement itself belongs to the game's publisher, so it is described here rather than reproduced.
