## Problem

New textbooks are expensive, while graduating students have books they no longer need.

## Approach

The team worked in an agile way, with user stories on a Kanban board, weekly progress reports and rotating presenters.
Each part of the product (catalogue, listings, registration, messaging, wishlist and fraud reporting) was its own
Django app.

## My part

- I built the messaging system, so buyers and sellers could arrange a sale inside the site.
- I linked each listing to the student who posted it, and allowed uploads only for signed-in users.
- I presented the team's weekly progress reports twice.

The messaging app has its own data model for conversations and messages, with pages to start a conversation, list
conversations, and send and read messages.
