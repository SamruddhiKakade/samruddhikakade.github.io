## Context

A library of recorded talks in audio and video, along with written texts, could not be searched by content, so finding
one topic meant going through whole recordings.

## Contribution

- Documented the stakeholder's requirements, wrote the design document and drew the wireframes.
- Developed audio extraction, speech-to-text transcription, transcript storage, indexing and search.
- Developed the web application in which a user chose the media, searched and opened the matching recording.
- Evaluated storage, speech and search services before selecting the technology stack.
- Presented the services tested and the reasons for the design.

## Lessons learned

- **Requirements and wireframes first.** Writing the requirements and drawing the wireframes before the code set out
  what the application had to do.
- **Test services before choosing.** Trying the candidate services, rather than choosing from their descriptions,
  grounded the technology choice in how each one performed.
