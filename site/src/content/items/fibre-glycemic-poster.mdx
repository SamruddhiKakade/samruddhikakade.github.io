## Question

Does adding extracted plant fibre to a cereal bar lower the rise in blood glucose and insulin after eating it?

## The study

A double-blind, randomised, crossover trial in healthy adults: each person ate the cereal bar both with and without
added fibre, on separate visits, and their glucose and insulin were measured over the next two hours.

<More>
The trial was run under ethics approval by a research team; the course provided its data to students. The poster
sets out the background, methods, results and conclusion, with tables of the participants' characteristics and the
composition of the two bars.
</More>

## Results

The bar with added fibre gave a lower glucose and insulin response than the bar without it.

The comparison is within each person (a crossover design), which controls for differences between people and makes
a small trial more informative.
