## Problem

Can the time and place of a crime report predict what kind of crime it is?

## Data

Chicago's public crime records for 2020 to 2022: 656,549 incidents across 33 crime types, described by community
area, type of location, day, month and year.

## Approach

<Flow
  label="The modelling pipeline"
  steps={['Encode and scale features', 'Compare seven model families', 'Stratified k-fold cross-validation', 'Grid search for the neural network']}
/>

I set up a fair comparison of seven classifiers (logistic regression, decision tree, random forest, naive Bayes,
k-nearest neighbours, support vector machine and a neural network), each evaluated the same way.

<More>
- **Stratified folds:** they keep the share of each crime type the same in every fold, so rare types are not missing from
  some folds.
- I rebuilt the cross-validation loop so that each fold trains on its own training split and is scored on its own
  held-out split, and set it up as an 80/20 split with three stratified folds.
- I added a grid search over the neural network: four layer shapes, two activation functions, two solvers, three
  regularisation strengths and two learning-rate schedules, 96 configurations in all.
</More>
