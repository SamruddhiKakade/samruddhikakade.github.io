## Context

Which crimes are most common in Chicago, how often do they lead to an arrest, and how have they changed in recent
years?

## Approach

The team analysed the City of Chicago's public crime records for 2020 to 2023 in R, in one shared R Markdown document
with a section for each question.

<More label="How the analysis plan worked">
The plan was a skeleton of the whole report, with a section for each question and notes on what each teammate would
add, set up before any analysis began. The team presented the results on 21 April 2024.
</More>

## Findings and limitations

- Theft (21.0% of records), battery (18.4%), criminal damage (11.7%), assault (9.0%) and motor vehicle theft (7.6%)
  were the five most common crime types.
- Arrest rates differed widely among them: 15.2% for battery and 10.3% for assault, but under 5% for theft, criminal
  damage and motor vehicle theft.
- Motor vehicle theft doubled from 2021 to 2022 and rose again in 2023.

<More label="Limitations">
- The records are crimes reported to and recorded by the police, not all crime.
- Cleaning dropped 9,533 duplicate or incomplete records, about 1% of the 2020 to 2023 data.
- The counts are not adjusted for population, and the analysis describes patterns without explaining their causes.
</More>
