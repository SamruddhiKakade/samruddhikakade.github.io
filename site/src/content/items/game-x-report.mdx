## Problem

A company is deciding whether to build "Game-X", a role-playing game, and how to charge for it. Leadership needs a
clear answer and the reasons behind it.

## Approach

I split the question into five research tasks covering the market, the audience, the costs and the technical
demands, researched each one, and brought the findings together into a single recommendation.

<More>
The final report follows the structure a decision-maker expects: a cover memo, an abstract, an executive summary,
the research methods, the results, conclusions and the recommendation, with references. It was the last of a series
of documents written during the course: a proposal, a progress report and then this recommendation report.
</More>

## Recommendation

Go ahead with the game, and drop the planned download fee in favour of in-game purchases, which the cost comparison
showed would earn more over time.

<More>
The pricing recommendation rests on a side-by-side comparison of a one-time download fee against recurring in-game
purchase revenue, set out in a table.
</More>
