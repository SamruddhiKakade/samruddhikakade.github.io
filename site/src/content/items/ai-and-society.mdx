## Question

Artificial intelligence may one day take on complex work. What would that mean for the people around it?

## Argument

The idea of made minds is old: myths of living statues and bronze guardians came long before computers. Modern AI
turns that idea into tools we use every day. The essay argues that AI could take on increasingly complex jobs, and
that this brings three risks to plan for: unemployment, bias built into systems, and the loss of human control.

The paper follows the IEEE conference format: abstract, index terms, a history of AI from myth to modern assistants
(with an illustration of an ancient coin showing Talos, the bronze guardian of Greek myth), a discussion of the risks,
and a conclusion.
