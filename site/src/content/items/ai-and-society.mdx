## Question

How has artificial intelligence affected society, and how might its continued development affect people?

## Argument

The idea of artificial minds is old: Greek myths tell of Pygmalion's sculpture brought to life and of Talos, a bronze
guardian of Crete. The field itself took shape with the Turing test, early theorem-proving software and the Dartmouth
workshop, and now reaches phones and homes as assistants. The essay weighed what AI already offers (support in
healthcare, dangerous and repetitive tasks taken over, higher productivity) against three risks: unemployment, bias
inherited from data, and loss of human control.

<More label="Structure of the paper">
The paper followed the IEEE conference format:

1. An abstract and index terms.
2. A history of AI from myth to modern assistants, illustrated with an ancient coin from Crete showing Talos.
3. Developments expected in the coming decade, such as self-driving cars, medical diagnosis and widespread assistants.
4. The effects on society: the benefits, then the three risks.
